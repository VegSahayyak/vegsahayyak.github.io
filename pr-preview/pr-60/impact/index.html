SDG 3.6: Vision Zero & Road Traffic Fatality Reduction

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), excessive and inappropriate speed contributes to nearly one-third of all fatal traffic crashes in high-income countries and up to one-half in low- and middle-income countries.

1. High-Precision Overspeed Prevention ​

The SDK's TelematicsEngine monitors velocity with sub-second accuracy using Exponential Moving Average (EMA) signal smoothing. When a vehicle exceeds safe thresholds, the SDK triggers immediate, tiered auditory and visual escalations before an accident occurs.

2. Expressway Rear-End Collision Defense ​

Vehicles traveling drastically below highway traffic flow present severe crash hazards. Veg Sahayyak incorporates configurable minimum speed corridors with intelligent 8 km/h stationary suppression to ensure drivers maintain safe highway momentum without generating false alarms at toll booths or traffic signals.

3. Eyes-on-the-Road HUD Mode ​

To combat distracted driving—the fastest-growing cause of road accidents—the :speed-ui module includes a pure OLED-black (#000000) Windshield HUD Mode. Utilizing Modifier.hudMirror(), driver speeds are projected onto the windshield glass, eliminating downward smartphone glances during night driving.

SDG 11.2: Inclusive Urban Mobility & Vulnerable Road Users

Urban transportation must be safe and accessible for all participants, particularly vulnerable road users (VRUs) sharing roads with heavy vehicles.

1. Micro-Mobility & Gig-Courier Protection ​

Delivery workers and two-wheeler operators face disproportionate road risks. Veg Sahayyak provides specialized presets for:

  • Motorcycles: Tuned for urban split-lane awareness.
  • E-Scooters: Restricted to municipal low-speed micro-mobility regulations (e.g., 20–25 km/h).
  • Bicycles: Low-speed filtering preventing erratic GPS drift.

2. Universal Accessibility (a11y) ​

The SDK enforces complete screen-reader compatibility across Compose UI components:

  • Full TalkBack semantics, content descriptions, and custom accessibility action labels.
  • Support for Android Switch Access and assistive input peripherals.
  • Dynamic font scaling and high-contrast OLED themes supporting elderly and visually impaired drivers.

SDG 12.2 & 13.3: Eco-Driving, Fuel Efficiency & Climate Mitigation

The faster a vehicle travels, the harder it has to fight aerodynamic wind resistance. Constantly speeding up and slowing down burns far more fuel than cruising at a steady pace. Staying in a consistent speed range keeps the engine running in its optimal efficiency zone, saving up to 15% on fuel and extending electric battery range.

The Eco-Cruising "Green Band" ​

Independent studies by the International Energy Agency (IEA) and the US Department of Energy confirm that maintaining steady cruising speeds and eliminating velocity oscillation reduces vehicular fuel consumption and emissions by 10% to 20%.

The SDK's Green Band provides clear, continuous feedback that trains commercial fleet drivers and commuters to remain in the optimal fuel-efficiency window, significantly lowering fleet fuel expenditures and associated tailpipe emissions ($CO_2$, $NO_x$, and $PM_{2.5}$).

Enterprise Governance: 100% On-Device Privacy ​

Many enterprise telematics platforms mine and monetize driver location data. In accordance with strict corporate Governance mandates and global data privacy standards (GDPR, California CCPA, India DPDP):

  • Zero Location Data Leaves the Device: All GPS coordinates, velocity calculations, and threshold evaluations occur purely on-device inside native execution memory. For commercial tiered billing, an aggregated heartbeat reports only total distance and duration; zero GPS coordinates or routes ever leave the device.
  • Offline Cryptographic Licensing: Verified offline via RSA-2048 JWT signatures and coupled to the native engine, eliminating telemetry beacons or external tracking calls.
  • Enterprise Audit-Ready: Easily passes corporate compliance reviews without requiring complex cloud data-sharing agreements or privacy impact assessments.
